# CMT artwork status

The quantitative CMT figures in `outputs/` are the authoritative frozen paper-specific artifacts because they were generated from the Stage A–F machine-readable outputs.

The repository also preserves an older six-figure combined-workflow set under the root `figures/` directory. Those figures remain useful provenance, but they represent the earlier broader study narrative (leakage controls, physics consistency, applicability domain, sodium triage, and DFT spot check) rather than the frozen CMT paper identity.

Any visually redesigned figure used for submission must preserve the exact underlying numerical source data. Style-only concept images or AI-redrawn approximate charts should not replace the quantitative repository figures unless they are rebuilt from the exact source tables and rechecked numerically.
